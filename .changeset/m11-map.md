A built-in map visual: countries shaded by one measure on the world map, keyed by ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes, with cross-filtering by country (ADR 0014). Its Natural Earth geometry loads lazily, only when a map draws; `topojson-client` decodes it. The builder offers "Add Map".
